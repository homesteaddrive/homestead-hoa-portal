HOMESTEAD HOMEOWNERS ASSOCIATION

2024 Annual Meeting Minutes

Public version · Meeting held July 22, 2024

Meeting Summary

The annual meeting was called to order at 6:12 p.m. and adjourned at 6:57 p.m. The prior year's meeting minutes were reviewed.

Officer Terms and Election

  • Officer term lengths were reviewed: President (three years), Treasurer (two years), and Secretary (one year).
  • Following a Treasurer vacancy, the membership elected a new Treasurer by unanimous vote.

Community Events

  • A community block party was planned for September 7.
  • The community discussed the recent yard sale and future seasonal decorating activities.

New Business and Community Maintenance

  • Residents discussed parking concerns and possible future updates to HOA governing documents. The discussion recognized the City’s role in code enforcement.
  • A proposal to provide dues-related recognition for board service was approved by unanimous vote.
  • Maintenance needs discussed included landscaping, playground upkeep, fence work, and the retention-pond area.
  • The community discussed protecting and maintaining shared facilities and encouraged residents to report suspected damage or safety concerns through appropriate non-emergency channels.
  • Community members were encouraged to participate in neighborhood stewardship and volunteer efforts.

2025 Dues

Annual HOA dues were kept at $150 for 2025.
