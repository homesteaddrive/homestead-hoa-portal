HOMESTEAD HOMEOWNERS ASSOCIATION

2025 Annual Meeting

Public notice regarding quorum

Quorum Notice

The Association did not establish a quorum at the 2025 annual meeting. Under Section 3.4.2.3 of the CC&Rs, a quorum is required to conduct Association business; accordingly, no Association business was conducted at that meeting.

This public notice is provided for community information. Questions or requests for Association records may be directed to the HOA.

Published as a public notice for the 2025 annual meeting.
